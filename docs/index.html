I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UC San Diego, advised by Professor Steven Swanson. My research spans multiple aspects of memory and storage systems for cloud infrastructure, including virtualization (vhost-NVMe, SC2'18), reliability (Spool, ATC'20), endurance (CSAL, EuroSys'24), sustainability (Sandman, SOSP'25), and hardware offloading (Latte, FAST'26).

Before my Ph.D., I was a Staff Engineer at Alibaba Cloud (2019 - 2023) where I contributed many innovations in cloud storage from concept to completion. Most of them have been deployed and currently benefit various storage products at Alibaba Cloud. A well-known product I led is D3C Instance that is designed for big data services with large-capacity and high-performance local storage. We designed this service with high-density QLC NAND flash and Cloud Storage Acceleration Layer (CSAL) in collaboration with Solidigm. Another significant project I made is ESSD-PLX which is the fastest cloud disk of Alibaba Cloud. To achieve the performance goal, I designed a distributed persistent memory cache from scratch.

I was also one of the founding members of SPDK China team at Intel where I interned from 2017 to 2018. I was fortunate to work with SPDK members on several storage projects (e.g., user-mode vhost and NVMe driver) and then applied these innovations to large-scale cloud systems like Alibaba Cloud.
