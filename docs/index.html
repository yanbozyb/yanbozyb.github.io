I am a forth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Steven Swanson. I am interested in computer systems for cloud and AI infrastructure. My current work focuses on rearchitecting storage systems for emerging hardware technologies (SC2'18, USENIX ATC'20, EuroSys'24), improving I/O processing efficiency (SOSP'25, FAST'26), and designing memory management beyond traditional boundaries (OSDI'26). I also collaborate with other researchers to address system-level challenges for AI agents (arXiv'26a, arXiv'26b).

Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at Alibaba Cloud (2019 - 2023) where I built Alibaba Cloud’s local storage product portfolio from the ground up as a founding member of the team. At Alibaba, I led the Cloud Storage Acceleration Layer (CSAL) project, which enabled the deployment of high-density QLC SSDs to replace HDDs in cloud storage systems. Another significant project I led was an ASIC/SoC co-designed DPU for cloud local storage, which received the Best Paper Award at USENIX FAST'26.
