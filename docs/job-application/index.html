Job Application

My research focuses on computer systems for cloud and AI infrastructure, with a particular emphasis on memory and storage systems. My goal is to make computer systems faster, more resource-efficient, and more reliable for traditional and agentic cloud workloads at large scale. This page brings together my application materials and representative publications.
