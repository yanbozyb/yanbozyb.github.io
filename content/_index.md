I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/), advised by Prof. [Steven Swanson](https://swanson.ucsd.edu/). My research spans multiple aspects of memory and storage systems for cloud infrastructure, including virtualization ([vhost-NVMe, SC2'18](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8567374)), reliability ([Spool, USENIX ATC'20](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/atc20-xue_0.pdf)), endurance ([CSAL, EuroSys'24](../paper/csal_eurosys.pdf)), sustainability ([Sandman, SOSP'25](../paper/sandman_sosp.pdf)), DPU design ([Ristretto, FAST'26](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/yang)), and software-defined memory ([RamRyder, OSDI'26](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/presentation/zhou-yanbo)).

Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at **Alibaba Cloud** (2019 - 2023) where I was a founding member of the local storage team. At Alibaba Cloud, I contributed many innovations in cloud storage from concept to completion. Most of them have been deployed and currently benefit various storage products. A well-known project I led was [Cloud Storage Acceleration Layer (CSAL)](../paper/csal_eurosys.pdf), which enabled high-density QLC NAND flash deployment in cloud data centers.
Another significant project I led was an [ASIC/SoC co-designed DPU](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/yang) for cloud local storage, which received the **Best Paper Award** at USENIX FAST'26.
