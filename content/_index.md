I am a forth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/), advised by Prof. [Steven Swanson](https://swanson.ucsd.edu/).
I am interested in computer systems for cloud and AI infrastructure. My current work focuses on rearchitecting storage systems for emerging hardware technologies ([SC2'18](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8567374), [USENIX ATC'20](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/atc20-xue_0.pdf), [EuroSys'24](https://yanbozyb.github.io/paper/csal_eurosys.pdf)), improving I/O processing efficiency ([SOSP'25](https://yanbozyb.github.io/paper/sandman_sosp.pdf), [FAST'26](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/yang)), and designing memory management beyond traditional boundaries ([OSDI'26](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/presentation/zhou-yanbo)). I also collaborate with other researchers to address system-level challenges for AI agents ([arXiv'26a](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00151), [arXiv'26b](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41508)).

Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at **Alibaba Cloud** (2019 - 2023) where I built Alibaba Cloud’s [local storage product portfolio](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/ecs/user-guide/local-disks) from the ground up as a founding member of the team. At Alibaba, I led the [Cloud Storage Acceleration Layer (CSAL)](../paper/csal_eurosys.pdf) project, which enabled the deployment of high-density QLC SSDs to replace HDDs in cloud storage systems.
Another significant project I led was an [ASIC/SoC co-designed DPU](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/yang) for cloud local storage, which received the **Best Paper Award** at USENIX FAST'26.
